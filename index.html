Drain It

Scoop the colorful water into buckets, empty the tank and reveal a beautiful photo hidden behind it.

Coming soon on the App Store

A splash of calm, one tank at a time

  • Tap the top blocks to scoop same-colored water into buckets.
  • Fill a bucket to 8 and it's carried away. Empty the tank to win!
  • Every level hides a new photo: collect them all in your Album.
  • Hundreds of levels with bigger tanks and new colors as you go.
  • Handy boosters: an extra bucket, freeze the timer, undo a move.
  • Free to play. No account needed. Your progress stays on your device.
