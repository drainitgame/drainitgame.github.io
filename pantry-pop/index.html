Pantry Pop

Drag jam jars, honey pots and pickles from shelf to shelf, line up three of a kind and watch them pop. Clear every shelf before the kitchen timer rings!

Coming soon on the App Store

Sort it, match it, pop it

  • Match three alike on a shelf and they pop in a burst of sparkles, with combos for quick chains.
  • 180 hand-tuned levels across six cozy rooms, from Grandma's Pantry to the Sweet Shop, then endless remixes.
  • New twists as you go: locked shelves, frozen goods that crack with every pop, and mystery bags.
  • Handy boosters: Magnet pulls a matching three, Shuffle re-deals the shelves, Freeze stops the clock.
  • Daily rewards, short levels and no account needed: your progress stays on your device.
  • Free to play. No account needed. Your progress stays on your device.
